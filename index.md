*Geology of Ham Hill - Bibliography*

This is a bibliography for the geology of Ham Hill, Somerset. It focuses on the Ham Hill Limestone Member (Hamstone) of the Bridport Sand Formation (Yeovil Sands) laid down in the Yeovilian Substage of the Toarcian Stage of the Lower Jurassic, but also includes other contextual references.
